03 / Know the conditions

Incentives have
important fine print.

The playbook outlines tax incentives and practical cross-border watch-outs. Confirm current criteria with the relevant agencies.

Qualifying manufacturing activities

5%

Corporate tax incentive

For 10 years with at least RM500 million in capital investment, or 15 years with at least RM1 billion. Land is excluded from the investment amount.

Eligibility and qualifying activities apply.

Playbook, printed p. 3

For qualifying knowledge workers

5%

Personal income tax

The playbook notes a 5% personal income tax incentive for knowledge workers.

Check the applicable requirements.

Playbook, printed p. 3

!

Before you expand

Keep these watch-outs in view.

  • Cross-border work can have tax consequences. Track where staff work and what they do. The JS-SEZ does not replace domestic tax rules or the conditions for treaty relief.
  • Related-party prices need support. Keep records to show that cross-border prices follow the arm’s length principle.
  • Incentives are not automatic. Align your activities and operating model with each scheme’s requirements, and monitor ongoing compliance.

Source: SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, printed pp. 14–17 and 26.
